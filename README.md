## About me

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at K.J. Somaiya College of Engineering, currently Lead Full-Stack Developer at Sojaru Corp and Captain of Team Vision, KJSCE's AR/VR student council. My work sits at the intersection of scalable web systems and immersive 3D — backend architecture on one side, Blender rigs and Unity builds on the other.

Recent focus has been on ML-integrated products (credit risk models, supply chain prediction) and a full 3D avatar pipeline for an emotional-wellness capstone project — MakeHuman meshes, facial shape keys for emotion and viseme control, and a GLB export chain feeding into a Three.js viewer.

I train seven days a week, competed in ISRO's Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon on exoplanet detection, and build small tools in my spare time — a published Chrome/Firefox extension is one of them.
